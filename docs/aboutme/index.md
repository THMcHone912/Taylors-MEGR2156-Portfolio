This page will primarily be used to communicate with others about assignments for Sophomore Design Lab, or MEGR 2156/2157.

Seeing the cars change, mainly from the Gen 6 car (I do not remember much of the Car of Tomorrow era) to the Next Gen car, or the Gen 7 car, has helped shape the career path I decided to take. With the introduction of the Next Gen car in 2022, and all of the injuries and questions surrounding the safety upon the mid-point of the inaugural Next Gen season pushed me into action my junior year of high school. Through Charlotte and their connections to NASCAR, I aim to continue to push the cutting edge of innovation while keeping and maintaining the old roots NASCAR is known for, and keeping the drivers safe as a whole so generations after can continue to enjoy the sport.  

Defending an engineering decision means being able to explain exactly why I chose a particular solution or design using calculations, engineering principles, and evidence. In my concentration, specifically NASCAR, I believe this is important because an engineering design can directly affect safety and performance of the driver. An example to help illustrate this is if I choose a certain safety feature or chassis design, I would need to be able to explain why it provides the right balance of crash protection, strength and weight. At this point in my engineering journey, I understand the basic idea of defending an engineering decision in theory, but I do not think I am fully confident in doing it with all of the calculations, engineering principles, and evidence from an entirety engineering standpoint. I can explain my reasoning of why I chose a particular design, but I still need to learn how to fully support my decisions with testing data, regulations, and technical evidence. My goal is to improve on this area throughout the semester.
